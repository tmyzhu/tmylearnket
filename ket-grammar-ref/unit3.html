规则速记：
短形容词（1-2音节）：加 -er / -est（tall → taller → tallest）
长形容词（3音节以上）：加 more / the most（exciting → more exciting → the most exciting）
特殊变化：big → bigger（双写g）；tidy → tidier（y改ier）
不规则：good → better → best；bad → worse → worst
填写正确的比较级或最高级
1. Batman is and than ordinary men. (strong / tall)
✅ stronger, taller — 单音节形容词直接加 -er。strong → stronger，tall → taller。
2. At 1.75 m, he was in fact the actor to ever play Batman. (short)
✅ shortest — 最高级用 the + 形容词 + est。short → the shortest（最矮）。
3. Dumbo has got ears than the other elephants. (big)
✅ bigger — big 是"辅音+单元音+辅音"结构，比较级双写末尾辅音再加 -er：big → bigger。
4. This new film is old Disney film. (exciting — 最高级)
✅ the most exciting — exciting 是多音节词，最高级用 the most + 形容词。
5. London is than my hometown. (crowded)
✅ more crowded — crowded 是双音节以上形容词，比较级用 more + 形容词。
6. My room is than yours. (tidy)
✅ tidier — 以辅音 + y 结尾的形容词，比较级把 y 改 i 再加 -er：tidy → tidier。
规则速记：
两者相同：as + 形容词原形 + as（Tom is as tall as Sue. 一样高）
两者不同：not as + 形容词原形 + as（Cats are not as noisy as dogs. 猫没有狗吵）
⚠️ as…as 中间用形容词原形，不用比较级！
改写句子，保持意思不变
1. Travelling by train is faster than by bus.
→ Travelling by bus is not as as travelling by train.
✅ fast — "火车比巴士快" 改为 "巴士没有火车快"，用 not as fast as。注意用原形 fast，不是 faster。
2. Tom is 1.65 m. Sue is 1.65 m, too.
→ Tom is as as Sue.
✅ tall — 两人身高相同，用 as tall as 表示"一样高"。
3. Dogs are noisier than cats.
→ Cats are not as as dogs.
✅ noisy — "狗比猫吵" 改为 "猫没有狗吵"，用 not as noisy as。
4. This phone costs €225. The bicycle costs €225, too.
→ The phone is as as the bicycle.
✅ expensive — 价格相同，用 as expensive as 表示"一样贵"。
5. Jo thinks Geography is easier than History.
→ Jo thinks History is not as as Geography.
✅ easy — "地理比历史容易" 改为 "历史没有地理容易"，用 not as easy as。
6. His character in the film is not as scary as Tim Burton's original drawing.
→ is scarier than the character in the film.
✅ Tim Burton's original drawing — "电影角色没有原画吓人" 说明原画更吓人，所以改写后主语是 Tim Burton's original drawing。
加强比较（差距很大）：much / far / a lot + 比较级
减弱比较（差距很小）：a bit / a little + 比较级
例：Sharks are a lot more dangerous than dolphins.（危险得多）
例：My brother is a bit taller than me.（高一点点）
选择正确的程度词（每题只选一个）
1. The film is only slightly better than the book.
✅ a little — "slightly（轻微地）"说明差距很小，选减弱词 a little 或 a bit。far / much / a lot 表示差距很大，与 slightly 矛盾。
2. I'm sure climbing Everest is extremely more difficult than it looks.
✅ far — "extremely（极其）"说明差距极大，选加强词 far（也可用 much / a lot）。a bit / a little 语气太弱，不符合。
3. Together we made the script slightly better.
✅ a bit — "slightly（稍微）"说明改进幅度很小，选减弱词 a bit 或 a little。much / far 表示大幅改进，语气过强。
4. Gorillas are ______ more dangerous than dolphins. （大猩猩危险得多）
✅ a lot — 大猩猩和海豚危险程度差别很大，选加强词 a lot（也可用 much 或 far）。a bit / a little 表示差距很小，不合适。
用括号中的词改写句子
1. Sharks are more dangerous than dolphins. (a lot)
→ Sharks are than dolphins.
✅ a lot more dangerous — 程度词放在比较级前面：a lot + more dangerous。
2. My brother is taller than me. (a bit)
→ My brother is than me.
✅ a bit taller — a bit 放在比较级 taller 前面，表示"高一点点"。
3. My new phone is better than my old one. (far)
→ My new phone is than my old one.
✅ far better — far 放在不规则比较级 better 前面，表示"好得多"。
4. Her hair is longer than yours. (a little)
→ Her hair is than yours.
✅ a little longer — a little 放在比较级 longer 前面，表示"长一点点"。
5. The film is more exciting than the book. (much)
→ The film is than the book.
✅ much more exciting — much 放在 more exciting 前面，表示"精彩得多"。
副词构成：形容词 + -ly（slow→slowly）；-y 结尾改 -ily（easy→easily）
不规则：good → well；fast → fast（不变）；hard → hard（不变）
副词比较级：多音节用 more + 副词（more carefully）；单音节加 -er（faster, harder）
不规则比较级：well→better；badly→worse；early→earlier
写出副词原形
careful →
✅ carefully — careful 直接加 -ly。
easy →
✅ easily — easy 以辅音+y 结尾，y 改 i 再加 -ly。
happy →
✅ happily — happy 以辅音+y 结尾，y 改 i 再加 -ly。
good →
✅ well — good 的副词是不规则的 well，不是 goodly！
fast →
✅ fast — fast 既是形容词也是副词，形式不变（不是 fastly）。
bad →
✅ badly — bad 直接加 -ly 变成副词 badly。
填写比较级副词形式
1. George writes than me. (clear)
✅ more clearly — clearly 是多音节副词，比较级用 more clearly。
2. You need to do your homework if you want good marks. (careful)
✅ more carefully — carefully 是多音节副词，比较级用 more carefully。
3. Can you speak , please? (slow)
✅ more slowly — slowly 是多音节副词，比较级用 more slowly。
4. You can come if you want. (early)
✅ earlier — early 的比较级是不规则的 earlier（y 改 ier），不用 more early。
5. You did even than me! (bad)
✅ worse — badly 的比较级是不规则的 worse，不是 more badly！
6. Martina speaks English than I do. (good)
✅ better — well 的比较级是不规则的 better，不是 more well！
7. Isabella always works than the other kids. (hard)
✅ harder — hard 是单音节副词，比较级直接加 -er：harder。

✅ far more difficult — difficult 的比较级是 more difficult；用 far 加强，表示"难得多"。

✅ cheaper — cheap 是单音节形容词，比较级加 -er：cheaper。
